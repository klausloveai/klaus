5. Issues that matter

  1. The personal guaranty is the whole case for collection. The SBA holds a perfected blanket lien on substantially all of Azucanela LLC’s personal property (UCC 220197594338, lapses May 27, 2027), and there are 38 recorded liens against the company, including open county tax liens. A judgment against the LLC alone is unlikely to be collected. Recovery depends on the handwritten lease ¶40(5) making Mr. Leiva responsible if the company cannot pay, and Mr. Leiva disputes it.
  2. Arbitration. Lease ¶35B (initialed by both sides) sends disputes not resolved by mediation to binding arbitration. Mr. Leiva has expressly reserved the right to enforce it. His references to mediation come from that clause, not from an offer to settle.
  3. Lease may be incomplete. The file index notes that pages tied to ¶40(3) and the ¶40 supplements box may be missing, while Mr. Leiva argues a separate guarantee section is incomplete and unsigned. Confirm with the client that we hold the complete signed lease.
  4. ADA claim ($4,750) is the weakest item. The signed addendum ¶34.D puts accessibility work on the landlord. The underlying file is Reyes v. Wu, 25STLC03900.
  5. Security deposit. The $5,000 was applied to the ADA settlement without a Civ. Code §1950.7 itemized statement; the claim is pleaded net of it.
