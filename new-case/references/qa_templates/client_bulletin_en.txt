⏳ CASE TIMELINE

Hello! So that you know what to expect, below is an outline of the key stages of your case and how long each one usually takes. Please use this timeline to see where your case currently stands (the average case takes 5-10 months from start to settlement).

1️⃣ Opening Your Claim (within 24 hours of signing)
Your retainer is signed and our representation begins. We open the claim, gather and review all case information, report the accident to both insurance carriers, and send letters of representation notifying them that you are represented. If you are having any physical discomfort, we will also help you get seen by a doctor as soon as possible.

2️⃣ Investigation (1-14 days or longer)
We submit the evidence most favorable to you so the carriers can determine liability, verify the at-fault party's policy limits, help you with vehicle repair and a rental, and follow your treatment. The stronger the evidence, the faster liability gets decided.

3️⃣ Medical Treatment (typically 2-5 months, depending entirely on your injuries and your doctor's orders)
We follow your treatment and recovery to make sure every injury is properly documented and treated, and that you recover as fully as possible. Once treatment is complete, we immediately collect and organize all of your medical records and bills.

4️⃣ Claim Negotiation
Our attorneys compile all of the evidence and send a formal demand to the insurance company. We then negotiate back and forth with the adjuster to obtain the highest possible settlement.
Estimated time: 1-3 months, depending on how reasonable the carrier is.

5️⃣ Pre-Litigation Settlement
Once the settlement amount is confirmed, we review it with you and obtain your approval. The insurance company sends the settlement check to our office, where it is held in our attorney trust account. We confirm all attorney fees and medical expenses, prepare a final disbursement breakdown for you, and release your check after you sign off.

6️⃣ Litigation Review
If the insurance company refuses to pay fair value, we will evaluate the case with you and discuss whether to file a lawsuit. Litigation can produce a higher recovery, but typically adds 6-12 months. The decision to file is yours. We will lay out the pros, cons, and estimated net recovery of both paths.

This is a general timeline and every case moves at its own pace. Please feel free to ask questions in the group at any time.

🏥 COOPERATING WITH YOUR MEDICAL TREATMENT

🔔 Note: Your treatment is how you recover, and it is also the single most important record in your case. Which doctors you see, what testing you have, and how long you treat are decided entirely by your symptoms and your doctor's orders. Our role is to document the process completely.

⭐ Please help us with the following
Keep your appointments: Please attend your scheduled follow-up visits on time.
Reschedule in advance if you cannot go: If something comes up, you are traveling, or you are returning to your home country, please notify the clinic and us ahead of time and move the appointment. Please do not simply miss it.
Do not stop treatment on your own: If you feel you need to stop or change doctors, please discuss it with your doctor first and let us know as well.
Report every symptom honestly: At each visit, tell your doctor about every area of discomfort, including anything that seems minor (dizziness, insomnia, numbness in the hands, anxiety, etc.). A symptom that is not written in the medical record does not exist as far as your case is concerned.
Report new symptoms right away: It is very common for symptoms to appear days or even weeks after a collision. If that happens, please see a doctor promptly and explain it.
Traveling or leaving the U.S. for an extended period: Please tell us in advance. Continuity of treatment, and later steps that may require your personal participation (signing documents, providing statements), need to be arranged ahead of time.

⚠️ Why gaps and missed visits matter
The insurance company reviews your treatment records page by page. If there is a long gap in the records, or if you missed multiple appointments, they will typically argue that your injuries had already resolved or that your complaints are unrelated to the collision, and use that to challenge your claim. So: if you are still in pain, please continue treating as directed; if you are feeling substantially better, please tell us so we can begin wrapping up.

📌 In one line: report your condition honestly, go when you can, and reschedule in advance when you cannot.

⚠️ IF YOU ARE INJURED AGAIN OR HAVE ANOTHER ACCIDENT DURING YOUR CASE

🔔 Note: This one is easy to overlook, but it matters enormously. Please tell us immediately.

📢 Situations you must report to us right away
Any new car accident (even a minor one, even if no police report was made);
Any injury at work, at home, at the gym, a fall, or a sports injury;
Any prior injury, prior accident, or prior treatment from before this collision that you have not already told us about.

🔍 Why you must tell us
The insurance company and defense counsel will obtain all of your medical records, not just the ones from this case. If there is a new injury in the middle that we do not know about, they will attribute all of your symptoms to that event, which directly undermines your recovery in this case. The same is true of prior injuries. It is far better for us to know and address them early than for the other side to find them first and argue the injury was pre-existing.

📌 If we know in advance, we can address it. If the other side finds it first, we are on the defensive. Full disclosure is always what protects you best.

💰 MEDICAL EXPENSES AND YOUR FINAL RECOVERY

🔔 Note: Two questions come up most often: "Do I have to pay for treatment out of pocket?" and "How much will I actually receive?" Here is how it works, so there are no surprises at the end.
You do not pay any attorney fees or case costs up front. Attorney fees are taken from the settlement at the end of the case, at the percentage stated in your retainer agreement.

🏥 How treatment is paid for during your case
If you have health insurance: We use your health insurance first, and the clinic bills your carrier directly.
Medical coverage on your auto policy (Med Pay / PIP): If your auto policy includes this coverage, it can be used to pay a portion of your medical bills.
Treating on a clinic lien: Some clinics allow accident patients to treat now and be paid at the end of the case out of the settlement. This is the clinic's own billing arrangement, worked out between you and the clinic.
Whichever applies, please photograph every bill, collection notice, and insurance EOB you receive and send it to the group. Do not pay them yourself, and do not ignore them.

💎 What you receive is the net amount, not the gross settlement
At the close of the case, the following are paid out of the gross settlement, in order: attorney fees, necessary case costs (obtaining records, reports, etc.), medical expenses and clinic liens, and any reimbursement owed to your health insurance or a government program (such as Medi-Cal or Medicare). What remains is the net amount paid to you.
Before any check is released, we will give you a complete, itemized breakdown to review line by line, and funds are only disbursed after you sign off. We will also work to negotiate reductions with each provider to increase your net recovery.

⚠️ About lawsuit advances and pre-settlement funding
There are companies and brokers who contact accident victims offering "settlement advances," "cash while you wait," or lawsuit loans. These contracts carry extremely high interest, and a large amount of principal and interest is taken off the top of your settlement first. Please do not sign any such agreement without telling us. Our firm cannot advance or lend you living expenses, but if you are in genuine financial difficulty, please tell us first and we will see whether we can help by adjusting the timing of certain steps or by working with the clinics on cost.

📢 Important: No attorney can promise a specific dollar amount early in a case. The value of a case depends on liability, the at-fault party's policy limits, the nature of your injuries, and the strength of your medical records. We will make each of those as strong as it can be.

💼 LOST WAGES

🔔 Note: Income you lost because you could not work after the collision is recoverable, but it must be supported by evidence, and that evidence has to be preserved as it happens. It is very difficult to reconstruct later.

📂 What to keep
Your doctor's off-work note: This is the most important item. Please ask your doctor to write how many days of rest are recommended, or what work you cannot perform, in the chart or in a separate note. Absences without a doctor's note are generally not accepted by the insurance company.
Proof of income: Pay stubs, and a letter from your employer confirming your wages and your time missed.
Self-employed, cash income, or rideshare/delivery work: Please provide tax returns, bank statements, and screenshots of your trips and earnings from the platform (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, etc.).
Time-off records: Emails or messages requesting leave, and screenshots of your work schedule.

⚠️ Please note
Report your missed days accurately and do not overstate them. Inflated numbers, once discovered, damage the credibility of the entire case and cost you far more than they gain.
Returning to work once you are physically able is best for you and for your case. It does not reduce the wage loss you have already incurred.
Even if you feel you only missed a few days, please keep the documentation and send it to us. We will determine whether to claim it.

🛡️ IF THE OTHER DRIVER HAS NO INSURANCE OR NOT ENOUGH

🔔 Note: California's minimum required auto liability limits are very low, and some at-fault drivers carry no insurance at all. For that reason we verify the limits on both sides.

⚖️ Two situations
The other driver is insured but the limits are too low: Once the at-fault policy is exhausted, if your own policy includes UM/UIM coverage (uninsured/underinsured motorist), you may claim the difference from your own insurance company.
The other driver is uninsured or fled the scene: Your recovery then depends primarily on your own UM coverage.

📌 We pull and review your own policy for this coverage during the investigation stage. If a UM/UIM claim needs to be opened, we will explain the process and its implications beforehand. This is coverage you already paid for, and making a legitimate claim on it does not affect your at-fault record.

⏱️ DEADLINES AND WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

🔔 Note: The law sets strict deadlines for bringing a claim. Once a deadline passes the right is lost, and no one can undo it. This is why we continually ask you for documents and records.

⚠️ About the deadlines
In California, personal injury claims generally have a two-year statute of limitations. If a government entity is involved (a city, public transit, a government vehicle), the deadline to present a claim can be as short as six months. Different rules apply to minors.
The deadline that applies depends on the specifics of your case. We track it for you, but only if we receive your materials in time.

📱 SOCIAL MEDIA DURING YOUR CASE (WeChat Moments / RED / TikTok / Facebook / Instagram, etc.)

🔔 Note: Please be careful about everything you post on social media while your case is open. Keep one thing in mind: what you post is not private.

⚠️ Why this matters
The at-fault party's insurance company and their attorneys routinely search a plaintiff's social media, often without your knowledge or consent. Even if your account is set to private or friends-only, the content can still be obtained through the legal process (discovery).

🔍 What they are looking for
Anything that appears inconsistent with your injuries, which they use to attack your claim, such as:
💡 Photos or videos of exercise, working out, dancing, travel, parties, lifting heavy objects, or hiking;
💡 Location check-ins, or comments like "I'm fine" or "all good";
💡 Any description of the accident, your injuries, your treatment, or the case itself.

✅ What to do
While the case is open, do not post anything about the accident, your injuries, your treatment, or the case;
Do not post photos or videos of physical activity, even ordinary everyday activity;
Do not communicate, argue, or discuss settlement privately online with the other driver, their family, or anyone connected to the case;
Do not accept new friend requests from people you do not know;
Set your accounts to private (but understand that private is not fully secure);
⚠️ Do not delete what you have already posted. Deleting posts after the fact can be treated as destroying evidence and creates a bigger problem. If you are concerned about something, tell us first rather than handling it yourself.

📌 In one line: while your case is open, if you are unsure, do not post. When in doubt, ask us first.

🚗 VEHICLE REPAIR AND RENTAL CAR

🔔 About this service: Assistance with your vehicle repair and rental car is a free additional service our firm provides. We will recommend the best available options so that you are not left without transportation.

🛠️ Your repair rights
Your choice of shop: You have the right to use any repair shop you trust. You are not required to use the insurance company's recommended network shop.
Following up: If the shop is slow or you have concerns about the repair plan, please contact the shop directly, and reach out to us any time for help.
Checking the work: When you pick up the vehicle, please inspect it carefully (paint color match, panel gaps, etc.). If there is a problem, contact the shop immediately and we can help.

⚠️ If your vehicle is totaled
If the vehicle is declared a total loss, you generally have two options:
Full payout: The vehicle is sold to the insurance company at current market value and goes through the total loss process.
Keeping the vehicle: The vehicle receives a salvage title, and the insurance company pays the vehicle's value minus the salvage value. The exact figure is set by the carrier.
Please note: once a vehicle is declared a total loss, only the insurance company can change that determination. We will assist you through the entire process until payment is received.

🚘 Rental car coverage and limits
Obtaining a rental: We ask the at-fault carrier to provide a rental vehicle during the repair period, within their policy limits.
What is covered: The carrier covers repair days only after liability is accepted. The class of vehicle and the daily rate are set by the carrier, and are generally based on a same or comparable class of vehicle.
What you pay: Reimbursement generally does not include taxes, fuel, or supplemental rental insurance.
Other transportation costs: Please keep your rental receipts, rideshare receipts (Uber/Lyft), and deposit receipts, photograph them, and send them to the group so we can request reimbursement.

💎 Personal property damaged in the vehicle
If a phone, sunglasses, laptop, or similar item was damaged in the collision, reimbursement can be requested from the at-fault party.
Please provide: photos of the damaged item, and the purchase or repair receipt (if you no longer have a receipt, a screenshot of the current online price of a comparable item will work).
What to do: Keep your originals and send copies to us so we can submit them together.

📢 Important: The services above are free additional services provided by the firm. If you have any questions during the repair or rental process, or need us to step in, please reach out to us and we will do everything we can to help.
