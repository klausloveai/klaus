## Body skeleton

```
Hi <FirstName>,

We have a new case referral in <City, State> — <N> clients, <one-line who they are>.
<Client A> was driving and <Client B> was his front-seat passenger.

<Case type> on <Month D, YYYY>. <Bare mechanism: who had right of way, what the other
vehicle did, how our client was struck. No fault conclusion, no carrier analysis.>

Our clients' own policy (<Carrier>, <State>) has <UM>, <UIM> and <PIP/MedPay>.
<Injury characterisation in plain words>, <treatment status>, and <the ask>.

Full details are in the attached lead sheet, along with the scene photos and the police card.

Please let me know if your team would be interested and I will send over the rest of the file.
```

Subject: `Referral_<Case type / hook> | <State>`
e.g. `Referral_3-Vehicle Intersection Collision, 2 Clients | Syracuse, New York`
Earlier accepted forms: `Referral_Rear-End Collision – 2 Adults, 1 Minor | Wisconsin`,
`Referral — Police PIT maneuver, innocent third party | Gov. Code claim due 12/20/2026`
(append a hard deadline to the subject when one exists).
