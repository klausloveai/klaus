## I-907 — edition 04/01/24, 7 pages

Prefix: `form1[0].#subform[N].`

### Part 1 — the person filing the request (pages 1–2)

| Item | Field | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | `Pt1Line3_GivenName[0]` / `Pt1Line3_FamilyName[0]` | name of whoever files: the attorney when the firm files, the petitioner when self-filed |
| 4 | `Part1_Line4_CompanyorOrganizationName[0]` | company named in the related case |
| 5 | `Part1_Line5_MailingAddress_InCareofName[0]`, `_StreetNumberName[0]`, `_Unit[0]`, `_AptSteFlrNumber[0]`, `_CityTown[0]`, `_State[0]`, `_ZipCode[0]`, `_Country[0]` | filer's mailing address |
| 6 | `Part1Line6_Checkbox[0]`=`/Y` (Yes) / `[1]`=`/N` (No) | mailing address same as physical address |
| — | `FormG28Attach[0]` = `/1` | select when a G-28 is attached |
| — | `AttyStateBarNumber[0]` | attorney bar number |
| 8 | `Part1_Line8_CheckBox` — `[2]`=`/A` · `[3]`=`/B` · `[0]`=`/C` · `[1]`=`/D` | see below |
| 9 | `Part1_Line9_CompanyIRSTaxNumber[0]` | company EIN |

**The four options are NOT in the order a naive text extraction suggests.**
Verified by reading each printed label's y-position and matching it to the
checkbox rect at the same height:

| Export | Label y | Printed option |
|---|---|---|
| `/A` (`[2]`, rect y≈559) | 561 | I am the **petitioner** |
| `/B` (`[3]`, rect y≈541) | 543 | I am the attorney or accredited representative **for the petitioner** |
| `/C` (`[0]`, rect y≈487) | 489 | I am the **applicant** |
| `/D` (`[1]`, rect y≈469) | 471 | I am the attorney or accredited representative **for the applicant** |

The two attorney options are interleaved with the two self-filer options, so
`/B` is *attorney for the petitioner* and `/D` is *attorney for the applicant* —
the opposite of what you get by pairing labels to checkboxes in reading order.

So on an O-1, where the underlying filing is a **petition** (I-129):

- firm files as attorney of record with a G-28 → **`/B`**
- petitioner files for itself, no G-28 → **`/A`**

Both are exactly what the filed precedents do (`/B` on Ziqing Shi and
YUQIAN TAO_O1A, `/A` on the self-petition Xiran Liu). An earlier version of this
file called the `/B` choice an error and told the reader to raise it with the
attorney — that was wrong, and it is a good illustration of why this skill says
to resolve every checkbox by rect against the printed label instead of by
reading order. `/D` would be the wrong box here.

## G-1650 — edition 06/03/25, 1 page, one per payment

Prefix: `form1[0].PG1[0].`

| Section | Field | Value | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applicant/Petitioner | `sfApplicantPetitionerName[0].GivenName[0]` / `.FamilyName[0]` | the name USCIS should associate the payment with (the precedents used the **beneficiary's** name on all six forms) | B |
| Account holder | `sfBankAccountHolderInfo[0].CB_AccountType[0]`=`/B` Business / `[1]`=`/P` Personal | `/B` | F |
| Account holder | `sfBankAccountHolderInfo[0].GivenName[0]` / `.FamilyName[0]` | `Shenqi` / `Cai` | F |
| Account holder | `sfBankAccountInformation[0].BusinessName[0]` | `Law Office of Shenqi Cai APC` | F |
| Account info | `sfBankAccountInformation[0].CB_CreditCardType[1]`=`/V` Checking / `[0]`=`/MC` Savings | `/V` | F |
| Account info | `sfBankAccountInformation[0].BankName[0]` / `.RoutingNumber[0]` / `.AccountNumber[0]` | firm bank details | F |
| Account info | `sfBankAccountInformation[0].AuthorizedPaymentAmt[0]` | the one fee this form pays, digits only (`530`, not `$530.00` — the form prints the `.00`) | — |
| Signature | `AuthorizationandSignature[0].SignatureOfApplicant[0]` | leave blank; Shenqi Cai signs, it is the firm's account | — |

`CB_CreditCardType` is misleadingly named: it is the Checking/Savings pair, not a
card type. `/V` is the leftmost box, which is Checking. Verified by rect and by
rendering a filled form.

**One G-1650 per fee, never combined** — USCIS requires the premium processing
fee to be submitted separately from other fees, and the Asylum Program Fee is a
separate line as well. See `fees-and-filing.md` for the three amounts.

Only one of the three stored precedent G-1650s has the full bank block; two are
missing routing, account, bank name, business name and the Checking box. An
incomplete G-1650 means USCIS cannot pull the money and the filing gets
rejected, so fill all of it every time regardless of what a precedent shows.
